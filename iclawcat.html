iClawCat

AI companion with a body — think, feel, act

Overview

iClawCat is an AI desktop companion that goes beyond chatting. It sees your emotions, guards your privacy, assists your workflow, and responds with physical movements and warmth. Combining a multi-modal AI core with motors, sensors, and a heating module, it's your AI co-pilot, hardware security key, and emotional companion — all in one cute cat-shaped form.

Form

Cat-shaped desktop companion — 10×8×12 cm

Connectivity

USB-C or Wi-Fi 6

AI Mode

Local-first, cloud-enhanced (optional)

OS Support

Windows / macOS (Linux coming soon)

Core Features

Smart Assistant

Voice commands execute actions — "open downloads", "find meeting notes". CPU throttling? It "pants" and warms up.

Security Guardian

Auto-lock when you leave, hide sensitive windows when someone peeks. Physical camera shutter for total privacy.

Creative Co-Pilot

Snap a hand-drawn sketch — AI renders it in cyberpunk, watercolor, or anime style. Brainstorm ideas, get mind maps.

Emotional Companion

Senses fatigue from sighs, typing force, and facial expressions. Responds by dimming the screen, playing soft music, and purring.

Meeting Assistant

Gesture-based slide navigation, mute reminders, time warnings. Never interrupt or forget to unmute again.
