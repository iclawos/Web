Vibe Chicken

A tiny biped you train with reinforcement learning

Overview

Vibe Chicken is an open-source, 25 cm bipedal robot built for one thing: learning. Fifteen degrees of freedom across articulated legs, neck, head and a grasping beak give it real physical agency — walking, kicking, grabbing, and standing up. The full stack — SDK, simulation, and RL training pipeline — is open source, with a tested sim-to-real workflow. Train new behaviors in simulation, deploy them on the robot, watch it strut.

Form

25 cm biped — 15 DOF, 800 g

Compute

Rockchip RK3566 + AI accelerator

Perception

Camera, LiDAR, 8×8 ToF, 2 IMUs

Control

15 Dynamixel servos, 50 Hz policy loop

Core Features

Real Bipedal Motion

15 joints across legs, neck, head and beak. Walks, kicks a ball, grabs objects, and stands up from the ground — real physical agency on the desk.

Reinforcement Learning

Train behaviors with RL in simulation and deploy via a tested sim-to-real workflow. Eight trained moves ship in the box — the rest are yours to teach.

Full Perception Stack

Camera + compact LiDAR + 8×8 time-of-flight depth matrix + two IMUs (body and head). It sees, senses depth, and knows its own posture.

Open Source, All the Way

SDK, simulation environments, RL training scripts, and tools are fully open source. What the robot runs is what you can read, fork, and retrain.

Playable Out of the Box

Pair via Bluetooth or plug in a gamepad. Phone app controls over BLE, live camera feed over WebRTC — no training required to have fun.

50 Hz Policy Loop

A dedicated control daemon drives all 15 servos and the IMU on a single synchronized bus at 50 Hz — safety limits, fall detection and joint clamps built in.

Hardware Specifications

Compute

  • Rockchip RK3566 + AI accelerator
  • 1 GB RAM, 32 GB storage

Actuation

  • 15 DOF: 5 per leg + 5 (neck / head / mouth)
  • Dynamixel servos on 1 Mbps shared bus

Perception

  • Camera (streams over WebRTC)
  • Compact LiDAR + 8×8 ToF depth matrix
  • 2 IMUs — body + head (<1 ms quaternion)

Connectivity

  • Wi-Fi (config via phone app)
  • Bluetooth LE for phone control
  • Gamepad support (USB / BLE)

Sensing & Feedback

  • Onboard mic — 40-band log-mel audio features
  • Voltage + temperature sensing per servo
  • Fall detection and thermal limits in safety layer

Power

  • Removable NP-F550 battery, 2600 mAh
  • ~1 hour runtime depending on use

Dimensions

  • 25 cm tall
  • ~800 g

OS & Runtime

  • Linux with signed, rollback-safe updates
  • Rust daemons — robotd (control), mediad, tofd, btd, configd
