Vibe Pods

AI natural language interface — your intelligent meeting companion

Core Innovation

Vibe Pods are a uniquely positioned AI natural language human-computer interface. More than just audio — they are an independent, intelligent AI natural language interface that also serves as your meeting assistant, capturing, transcribing, and analyzing every conversation. With Bluetooth + WiFi dual-channel connection, they provide seamless voice interaction for Vibe Code on your computer.

Dual-Channel Recording

Earbuds capture near-field voice, while the charging case with 6 microphones handles far-field pickup up to 10 meters. Full 3D stereo recording for every scenario.

Cloud AI Engine

Real-time transcription in 120+ languages with 95%+ accuracy. Automatic meeting summaries, mind maps, speaker identification, and team knowledge base sync.

Dual Connection

Bluetooth + WiFi — simultaneously connected to your phone and computer. Voice commands for Vibe Code without switching devices.

Professional Recording

Near-Field — Earbuds

Dual-mic beamforming on each earbud captures your voice with crystal clarity. Ideal for one-on-one conversations, phone calls, and focused dictation.

Far-Field — Charging Case

6 high-sensitivity microphones in the charging case capture a full 360° soundscape up to 10 meters. Perfect for roundtable meetings, lectures, and conference rooms.

3D Stereo Recording

Dual-channel synchronized recording creates spatial audio reconstruction. Know who spoke, from where, and in what order — as if you were there.

Intelligent Noise Suppression

AI-powered noise cancellation removes air conditioning, fan hum, and background chatter. Only the voices that matter are transcribed.

AI Capabilities

Real-Time Transcription

120+ languages and dialects supported. 95%+ accuracy with live on-screen text as people speak.

Simultaneous Translation

Real-time interpretation across languages. Perfect for international meetings and multilingual conferences.

Meeting Summaries

Auto-generated structured summaries with key points, decisions, and action items — delivered seconds after the meeting ends.

Mind Maps

Complex discussions transformed into visual mind maps with a single tap. Navigate ideas at a glance.

Speaker Recognition

Voiceprint-based speaker diarization identifies and labels each participant. Know who said what, every time.

Cloud Knowledge Base

All transcripts synced to the cloud for team-wide search and reuse. Your institutional memory, always accessible.

Design & Wearability

Open-Ear Design

Non-invasive open-ear design at just ~7g per earbud. Comfortable for all-day wear without fatigue or occlusion.

Long Battery Life

5 hours of continuous recording, 25 hours with the charging case. Music playback extends to 40 hours.

Local Storage

Quick Charge
